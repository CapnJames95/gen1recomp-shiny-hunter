# Differences from vanilla

Automate encounters and stop for shinies or chosen targets.

Supports Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, FireRed and LeafGreen.

Extends shiny odds to static encounters, new starters, gifts, eggs and roamers. Adds an optional Dual Screen odds toggle and hold-L shiny shortcut for Wild Pokémon encounters. Fixed events and existing Pokémon remain unchanged.

See [README](README.md) for features, controls and limitations.
